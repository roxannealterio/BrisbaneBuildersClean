Cleaning townhouse and duplex developments

Across Mount Gravatt, Sunnybank, Annerley and Moorooka there are a lot of townhouse projects, duplexes and small unit developments. Builders need every dwelling cleaned to the same standard, often to a tight handover date.

We can clean a whole development in stages as each unit is finished, so you are never waiting on the cleaners to hand over.

What we take care of

  • Unit-by-unit cleans to match your handover program
  • Garages, driveways and courtyards
  • Windows, sliding doors and tracks
  • Kitchens, bathrooms and laundries ready to use
  • Consistent finish across every dwelling

Our services in this area

  • Builder cleans for new homes and townhouses
  • Renovation cleans for extensions and makeovers
  • Rough and sparkle cleans at each stage of a build
  • Pre-listing cleans before photos and open homes
  • External cleans and house washing

How booking works

Send us the address, the type of clean and roughly when you need it. We come back with a fixed, itemised quote, usually within one business day. A 25% deposit locks in your date and comes off the final invoice. If anything on our scope is not up to standard, tell us within 48 hours and we come back and fix it free.
