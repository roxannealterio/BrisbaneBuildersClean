External cleans

House washing & external cleans in Brisbane

Walls, windows, driveways and decks cleaned properly, for new builds and homes getting ready to sell.

Why the outside matters

The outside of a property is the first thing buyers, tenants and inspectors see. After a build, render, mortar and concrete splatter are left on paths and walls. On older homes, dirt and mould build up on walls, eaves and outdoor areas. Either way, a clean exterior makes the whole property look better.

House washing

  • House walls and eaves washed
  • Driveways, paths and patios pressure cleaned
  • Decks, fences and outdoor areas
  • Soft wash or pressure clean, chosen to suit each surface
  • Ideal before listing photos or an open home

External builder cleans

  • Outside windows, frames, tracks and sills
  • Render, mortar and concrete splatter removed
  • Driveways, paths and garage floors
  • Balconies, alfresco areas and outdoor tiles
  • Builder's rubbish and debris cleared from the site

Who we work with

Builders who want the outside handed over as clean as the inside. Real estate agents getting a listing ready for photos. Homeowners getting ready to sell, or who just want the house looking fresh again.

How much does house washing cost?

Every house is different, so we quote per property. The price depends on the size of the home, how many storeys it has, what surfaces need cleaning and how easy access is. Send us the address and a few photos and we will give you a fixed quote.

Areas we cover

We cover Brisbane's inner south and east, from South Brisbane and West End to Coorparoo and Camp Hill, Mount Gravatt and Sunnybank, and Carindale, Wynnum and Manly.

How booking works

Send us the address, the type of clean and roughly when you need it. We come back with a fixed, itemised quote, usually within one business day. A 25% deposit locks in your date and comes off the final invoice. If anything on our scope is not up to standard, tell us within 48 hours and we come back and fix it free.
