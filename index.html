Who we are

A spotless finish,
ready for handover.

Once the trades are off site, we take care of the detail that makes a project truly ready. Our team removes every trace of the build, including the fine construction dust, render splatter, paint, stickers and marks, then cleans each surface to a professional standard, so the space is spotless, safe and ready for inspection, handover or sale.
